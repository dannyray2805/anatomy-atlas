## Relations

- Uberon records this structure as: "The median sacral vein (or middle sacral veins) accompany the corresponding artery along the front of the sacrum, and join to form a single vein, which ends in the left common iliac vein; sometimes in the angle of junction of the two iliac veins." (uberon).
- The median sacral vein (or middle sacral veins) is a vein of the abdomen. (wikipedia-median-sacral-vein)
- Its node names are `VH_M_median_sacral_vein` on the male donor's model, and `VH_F_median_sacral_vein` on the female donor's model (hubmap-hra-glb).
