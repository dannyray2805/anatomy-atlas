## Relations

- The small cardiac vein, also known as the right coronary vein, is a coronary vein that drains parts of the right atrium and right ventricle of the heart. (wikipedia-small-cardiac-vein)
- Its node names are `VH_M_small_cardiac_vein` on the male donor's model, and `VH_F_small_cardiac_vein` on the female donor's model (hubmap-hra-glb).
