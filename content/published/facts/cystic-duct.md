## Identity

- The structure `cystic-duct` is labelled "cystic duct" in Uberon (uberon).
- Its Uberon identifier is `UBERON:0001152` (uberon).
- On the Visible Human female donor body it is carried as the mesh node `VH_F_cystic_duct`
  (hubmap-hra-glb).

## Relations

- UBERON:0001152 is "the tubular structure that conducts gall bladder contents from the gall bladder
  to the common bile duct" (uberon).
- "The cystic duct is the duct that (typically) joins the gallbladder and the common hepatic duct;
  the union of the cystic duct and common hepatic duct forms the bile duct ... It is the distal
  continuation of the neck of the gallbladder" (wikipedia-cystic-duct).
- In this atlas it hangs directly off the `body` root row (layer `organ`) (hubmap-hra-glb).
