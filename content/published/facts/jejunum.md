## Relations

- "The jejunum is the second part of the small intestine in humans and most higher vertebrates,
  including mammals, reptiles, and birds. Its lining is specialized for the absorption by enterocytes
  of small nutrient molecules which have been previously digested by enzymes in the duodenum"
  (wikipedia-jejunum).
