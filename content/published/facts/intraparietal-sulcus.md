- Uberon records this structure as: "The intraparietal sulcus (IPS) is located on the lateral surface of the parietal lobe, and consists of an oblique and a horizontal portion. Its principal functions are related to perceptual-motor coordination (for directing eye movements and reaching) and visual attention.
