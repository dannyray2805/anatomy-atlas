## Identity

- The structure `vertebral-column` is labelled "vertebral column" in Uberon (uberon).
- Its Uberon identifier is `UBERON:0001130` (uberon).
- On the Visible Human donor bodies it is carried as the individually named vertebrae, e.g.
  `VH_M_cervical_vertebra_1` ... `VH_M_lumbar_vertebra_5` (hubmap-hra-glb).

## Relations

- UBERON:0001130 is "a subdivision of skeletal system that consists of all the vertebra and
  associated skeletal elements and joints in the body" (uberon).
- "The spinal column, also known as the vertebral column, spine or backbone, is the core part of
  the axial skeleton in vertebrates ... The spinal column is a segmented column of vertebrae that
  surrounds and protects the spinal cord. The vertebrae are separated by intervertebral discs in a
  series of cartilaginous joints" (wikipedia-spinal-column).
- In this atlas it hangs directly off the `body` root row (layer `skeleton`) (hubmap-hra-glb).

## Presence

- Present in BOTH Visible Human donor bodies — 24 named vertebrae on the male body and 25 on the
  female body (hubmap-hra-glb).
- VARIANT: the female donor's source names six lumbar vertebrae (`VH_F_lumbar_vertebra_6`) rather
  than five — a lumbarisation / transitional-vertebra variant. It is reported here exactly as the
  source names it and is mapped to this same row (hubmap-hra-glb).
- This is the spine ONLY. Neither donor's full articulated skeleton (skull, ribs, limb bones) is
  published, so the spine is the whole of the `skeleton` contribution on these bodies
  (hubmap-hra-glb). The Reference Atlas body carries a complete skeleton, but as a whole layer that
  is not mapped per bone (z-anatomy).
