## Identity

- The structure `superior-rectal-vein` is labelled "superior rectal vein" (uberon).
- Its Uberon identifier is `UBERON:8920053` (uberon).
- Uberon also records the synonym(s) "Superior Anorectal Vein" for this term (uberon).

## Relations

- Uberon records this structure as: "A vein that is a tributary of the inferior mesenteric vein and drains the upper two thirds of the rectum and upper anal canal." (uberon).
- The inferior mesenteric vein begins in the rectum as the superior rectal vein (superior hemorrhoidal vein), which has its origin in the hemorrhoidal plexus, and through this plexus communicates with the middle and inferior hemorrhoidal veins. (wikipedia-superior-rectal-vein)
- Its node names are `VH_M_superior_rectal_vein` on the male donor's model, and `VH_F_superior_rectal_vein` on the female donor's model (hubmap-hra-glb).

## Presence
