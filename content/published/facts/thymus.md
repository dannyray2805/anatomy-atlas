## Relations

- UBERON:0002370 is "an anatomical structure of largely lymphoid tissue that functions in
  cell-mediated immunity by being the site where T cells develop" (uberon).
- "The thymus is a specialized primary lymphoid organ of the immune system. Within the thymus,
  T cells mature. ... The thymus is located in the upper front part of the chest, in the anterior
  superior mediastinum, behind the sternum, and in front of the heart. It is made up of two lobes,
  each consisting of a central medulla and an outer cortex, surrounded by a capsule"
  (wikipedia-thymus).
